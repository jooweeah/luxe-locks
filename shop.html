Wig Care Tips

Keep your investment looking flawless

01

Gentle Washing

Wash every 7-10 wears with sulfate-free shampoo. Always detangle before washing.

02

Proper Storage

Store on a wig stand or mannequin head to maintain shape and prevent tangling.

03

Heat Protection

Always use heat protectant before styling. Keep temperatures below 350°F.

04

Night Care

Wrap in a silk scarf or bonnet before bed to reduce friction and extend longevity.
