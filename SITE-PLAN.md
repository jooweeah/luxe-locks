# Luxe Locks - Site Plan

## Business Overview

Luxe Locks is a premium hair salon and wig boutique located in Downtown Orlando, specializing in luxury installs, custom wigs, protective styling, and confidence-boosting transformations. The brand blends high-end beauty with approachability, offering both in-salon services and curated wig products.

**Target audience:** 
- Women ages 20–50
- Professionals, creatives, and students in the Orlando area
- Clients seeking protective styles, hair loss solutions, or luxury hair experiences
- Style-conscious customers who value quality, personalization, and comfort

**Brand personality:** 
- Luxurious but welcoming
- Confident, empowering, and feminine
- Modern, polished, and detail-oriented
- Trustworthy and client-focused

## Page Structure

### 1. Home

- Hero section with brand statement and call-to-action (Book Now)
- Highlights of signature services and wigs
- Brief brand story and value proposition
- Testimonials or client transformations
- Location and quick contact info

### 2. Services

- Detailed list of salon services (installs, braids, treatments, styling)
- Wig services (customization, installs, maintenance)
- Pricing ranges and service descriptions
- Booking call-to-action

### 3. Wigs & Shop

- Product gallery of available wigs
- Categories (lace fronts, closures, custom units)
- Care tips and quality assurances
- Option to inquire or purchase (online or in-store)

### 4. About & Contact

- Brand story and mission
- Stylist background and expertise
- Salon location and hours
- Contact form, booking link, and social media links

## Design Direction

**Colors:**

- Soft neutrals ( #F7F2ED, #E8DCD2, #B8AFA6 )
- Accents of black ( #0F0F0F ) and gold ( #C9A24D ) for luxury

**Typography:**

- Elegant serif for headings (luxury feel)
- Clean sans-serif for body text (readability)

**Overall aesthetic:** Modern luxury with a feminine edge. Clean layouts, soft lighting, high-quality imagery, and subtle glam touches that feel upscale but not intimidating.
